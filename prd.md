<h1>Background &amp; Strategic Fit</h1>
<p>The app has been facing issues in screen freezing and slow loading for over 5 seconds on both Android and iOS mobiles. The case study aims to reduce the number of screens by optimising the payment process, to improve the number of recharge completions and decrease the drop-off rate, aligning with the company vision of improving physical card sales.</p>

<h1>Problem Statement</h1>
<p>Reviews across platforms observed that the payment process is overwhelming the users, and drop-off is recorded due to user dissatisfaction among the DMRC regular and occasional commuters via the Airtel Thanks app.</p>
<h1>Goals &amp; Objectives</h1>
<p>The goal is to reduce information overload, simplify the design, and provide smooth user onboarding to improve engagement. It involves UI and UX efforts to align the app with the business goal and product vision, as mentioned below:</p>
<ol>
<li>UI retouches to make the icon on the homepage stand out.</li>
<li>Reduce the number of screens involved in the payment process.</li>
<li>Integrate the progress status bar into the transaction process.</li>
</ol>
<h1>Empathy &amp; Research</h1>
<h3><strong>Observation</strong></h3>
<p>8 users were asked to recharge the card using both the icon on the home screen and the search query result. Observations noted slow loading, and the entire payment process took a median time of ~267 secs unassisted, causing frustration.</p>
<h3><strong>Reviews</strong></h3>
<p>Probed the internet and got reviews on Reddit, X, and the Google Play Store criticising the app&rsquo;s UX, criticising the pop-up ads, screen freezing/excess loading time, and discrepancy in app vs card balance.</p>
<h3><strong>Interviews</strong></h3>
<p>Interviews were held for 3 members aged 18-55, where frustrations were recorded regarding the user journey from the home screen till the payment confirmation interface. The pain points were noted and duly delivered in the prototype iterations.</p>
<h3><strong>Competitor Analysis</strong></h3>
<p>Three problems were identified: slow loading time, excess screens, and information overload. Upon conduct of competitor analysis, it was concluded that the other UPI TRAPs (Third-Party Application Providers), like the dedicated DMRC Saarthi app and other banking apps like Paytm and Pine Labs, were able to support NCMC recharge facilities with a smoother and quicker interface supporting standard information exposure. Opportunity arose to declutter the screens while improving the UI through clarity in texts and UX via simplifying the payment screens involved.</p>

<h2>GTM</h2>
<ul>
<li>The Airtel Thanks app will be required to update on the iOS app store/Google Play Store immediately after the features are rolled out.</li>
<li>A launch on feature addition will be conducted on the social media accounts of both Airtel and the dedicated Airtel Payments Bank, namely on Instagram and X.</li>
<li>A push notification will be sent out for users approximately 2 days after the launch, with a cycle of 1 week, to not bombard the users while informing them to update the app and adopt it accordingly.</li>
<li>An infographic will be embedded in the mobile app and on the website for announcements in the header of the landing page/screen.</li>
</ul>
